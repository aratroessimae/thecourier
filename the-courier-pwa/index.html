Every school community is shaped by the ideas, kindness, and determination of its learners. At Eastern Samar National Comprehensive High School, students bring different experiences and perspectives to a shared goal: making their school a place where everyone can learn, contribute, and thrive.

From classroom collaboration to campus initiatives, meaningful change often begins with small actions. Listening to others, caring for shared spaces, and approaching challenges with curiosity can help transform everyday school life.

The Courier

The online publication of Eastern Samar National Comprehensive High School.

News. People. Ideas. A Brighter Eastern Samar.

The Courier is envisioned as a student-centered space for responsible campus journalism—sharing verified school news, thoughtful features, sports coverage, and editorial cartoons. It aims to inform the school community, amplify student perspectives, and encourage respectful public discussion.

This prototype uses placeholder content and is not connected to a live publishing server. Before launch, designate editors, establish an editorial review process, confirm image permissions, and publish the school's official contact and privacy information.
